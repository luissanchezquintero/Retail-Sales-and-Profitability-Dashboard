# Retail-Sales-and-Profitability-Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard built using Power Query and PivotTables to analyze revenue, gross profit, sales channels, product performance, and year-over-year trends.
Tools: Microsoft Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and Slicers.

2018 vs 2017 Highlights
YoY growth was strong increasing by +72.3%. The growth was driven by higher order volume and not larger purchases. This is shown by the average order value (AOV) declining by 5.2%, from $2,263 to $2,144. Action: analyze quantity per order and product mix to understand why AOV declined despite strong revenue growth.
Computers delivered approximately 42.5% of the total $3.13M gross profit increase across all categories. Revenue for computers grew 102.3%, making it the largest contributor to both revenue and gross profit. Action:investigate computer product demand, best-selling models, and inventory availability to determine whether the category presents opportunities for further growth.
